## Cultivation Methods

### Living Soil
We recycle all food waste using Bokashi bins and a worm farm composter. At the start of spring each year, we work the organic waste into the soil, adding valuable nutrients, minerals and microorganisms for the new season of growing.

### Rainwater Harvesting
Cape Town was one of the first cities to almost run out of water in 2018. We collect rainwater and store 13,500 litres for household and growing usage. The water is pumped through a triple filter with UV light to ensure a neutral pH balance.

### Companion Planting
Companion planting is the practice of pairing certain varieties of flora to naturally deter pests. We specifically plant basil with every cannabis plant we grow. We also use sulphur and diatomaceous earth for natural pest management.

### Worm Tea
We brew worm compost tea every two weeks throughout the growing season using an aerobic compost tea system. The system efficiently extracts and amplifies beneficial microorganisms from the compost. It must be bubbled for 48 hours for best results.

## Harvest Phases

### Flushing
We start 10 days before harvest with flushing the cannabis plants. Flushing involves running water through the soil medium to remove traces of minerals and other nutrients, forcing the plant to consume stored nutrients in its final week.

### Drying
We hang the plants in darkness in the drying room, kept at 16–18°C with a relative humidity of 55–60%. A fan on low speed ensures decent air circulation to prevent mould. Once the branches snap, they are dry enough to jar.

### Dry Trimming
After about 1–2 weeks on the drying nets, most moisture is gone. We do a very tight trim on our Standard Grade and Topshelf buds, which yields a lot of powdery goodness that we package as our Budget Grade.

### Curing
We place the dry flowers into airtight glass jars. During the first three weeks, we open the jars for ten minutes every day in a dark room. We taste the flowers every week to check their ripeness. After about 2 months we start to package and label — Homegrown Happiness!

## Grading System

**Quads (AAAA)** — The best of the best.
Premium cannabis: well cured, pungent odour, sharp flavour, smooth clean-smelling burn and potent smoke. Hand-selected best-trimmed buds.

**Topshelf (AAA)** — Quality fitting for connoisseurs.
Aesthetically pleasing structure, desirable terpene aromas and flavours, high cannabinoid content. Dense, well-formed bud. No harsh aftertaste.

**Standard (AA)** — Smaller buds than Topshelf, but still top quality.
Same characteristics as Topshelf but less bud density. Still excellent quality with high THC potency.

**Preground (A+)** — Save time with fine-sieved convenience.
A blend passed through a sieve to remove unwanted plant matter like stalks and seeds, giving you a smooth smoke.

**Budget Blend (A)** — Perfect for making edibles.
A tight trim of Standard and Topshelf buds, yielding powdery goodness. Includes some small popcorn buds.

## Packaging

Glass packaging is preferred. Customers return the jars, which are sanitised and recycled for the next season's products. Glass is non-porous and impermeable, preserving taste and aroma for well over a year.

## Six Cats & Ash's Green Fingers

Six Cats is the outlet for what Ash calls his "green fingers" — the deeply satisfying practice of growing something from seed to harvest. This isn't just about cannabis; it's about cultivation as craft, patience as practice, and a relationship with nature that grounds everything else.

Ash has been growing for nearly 20 years. Six Cats formalised that passion into a club with a community, a brand, and a mission. The green fingers are the same hands that paint neon faces at festivals — both practices require the same qualities: patience, attention to detail, sensitivity to the subject, and a deep respect for the process.
