## Cycling

Ash's longest and deepest sporting relationship. The bicycle is identity.

### Racing History
- Racing bicycles since **1994** (age 13)
- First provincial mountain bike race in **1995**
- Represented **Western Province cross country mountain bike team**
- Western Province cross country mountain bike colours:
  - **1997:** 3rd overall
  - **1998:** 1st overall (Western Province champion) — earned half colours at Paarl Boys High
  - **1999:** 3rd overall
- At Daemelin College (1999), trained extensively for Western Province and South African mountain bike championships
- Created a cycling portfolio to try to get sponsorship as a kid

### Bike Packing & Touring
- Bike packing since **2012**
- Started riding to festivals in **2018**
- Evolved from racing into a fundamentally different relationship with the bike — endurance, self-sufficiency, meditation, adventure

### The Touring Kit
- **Gravel bike** (primary touring bike)
- **Arkel pannier bags**
- **Tubus racks**
- **Bike bag** (top tube, next to handlebars)
- **Phone mount**
- UV paints, brush kit, mirror stand (when heading to festivals)
- Camping gear
- Total weight: **40kg** (bike + gear)

### Festival Cycling Pilgrimages

**Origin Festival (4 times: 2020, 2022, 2023, 2026):**
- Route: Woodstock, Cape Town → Sir Lowry's Pass → Grabouw → Elgin Valley → Houwhoek Pass → Helderstroom
- ~150km one way through mountain passes in the Western Cape
- 2026 birthday ride stats: ~300km round trip, 40kg bike pack, 3,200m climbing, 14 hours of riding, top speed over 75km/h
- 2023: Birthday sash ride — cycled the whole way to Peregrine Farmstall in Grabouw wearing a birthday sash
- 2022: First year back after COVID — felt like coming home

**Cape Town to Stormsvlei:** 185km in one go to party for four days — "pretty insane"

**Bangkok to Chiang Rai to Chiang Mai:** Rode from Bangkok to Chiang Rai for a festival, spent the weekend at the festival, then rode on the Monday to Chiang Mai. People at festivals don't believe he's going to ride home — "when they see me riding out of the festival on the Monday morning, they believe me then."

**European festival cycling:** Cycled through Germany, Czech Republic, Austria to reach festivals. Arriving at festival gates with panniers full of UV paint.

### Thailand Cycling
- Over **7,000km** touring on bicycle in Thailand
- Hua Hin to Phuket: nearly 900km in 6 days for friend Pierre Vocat's wedding, then 1,100km on the return. Got saddle sores with lasting scars.
- Keep daily distance to 80–120km max in Thailand due to humidity

### Other Notable Cycling Adventures
- 300km bike trip in California: San Francisco to Point Reyes and back (2014)
- Two trips in the Netherlands: nearly 800km total
- Munich to Amsterdam: 1,000km in 10 days
- Bike touring in Bali

### Daily Cycling in Berlin
- Primary transport, meditation, and creative process
- The flat, endless Berlin streets free the mind
- Görlitzer Park to Tempelhof, Spree canal to Tiergarten — ideas form with every pedal stroke
- The fairy lights bike: became a recognisable figure at Berlin open-airs

### Proudest On-the-Road Repair
Riding with a road bike in a trailer with a 14-inch tyre. Got a flat. The tube wasn't something replaceable in Thailand. Had tyre sealant — took the valve out, got the sealant into the tube, pumped it up, and sealed the puncture. Resourcefulness on the road.

## Festival Endurance

Multi-day festivals require a specific kind of fitness:
- Dancing for hours in heat or cold
- Setting up and painting all day, dancing all night
- Cycling to and from the festival with full gear
- Hearing protection: **Loop Experience 2** ear plugs
- Multi-day events: only sleeps a few hours a night — "you recover after the festival"
