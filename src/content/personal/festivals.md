# Festivals & Psytrance Culture

## Timeline

### 1999 — The Beginning
- **First festival:** Vortex Easter weekend festival (1999)
- **Second festival:** December Vortex 1999 — first transformative experience with Lucy
- **Millennium Vortex:** Legendary party for New Year's 1999/2000
- Ash was seventeen at the December Vortex. He was wearing a yellow suit he'd found at a charity shop, and for the first time in his life, the world made perfect sense. Not the world outside — that place had never made sense. The world inside. The one that runs at 140 BPM and communicates in bass frequencies.
- By morning they were calling him the Chicken Man.

### Early 2000s — The Scene Years
- Discovered a love for dancing at festivals
- Conceptualised the idea of dressing up at parties
- **Costume evolution:**
  1. Yellow suit — "the Chicken Man"
  2. Red suit
  3. White and black cow suit
  4. Brown and beige cow suit — became known as "the Cow Man" for many years (people still ask if he's the guy who used to dress as the cow man)
- Became very well known at parties because of the unique outfits
- Psytrance buddies became a staple in life
- In the 2000s, attended **2 to 4 festivals a month** in Cape Town
- This wasn't casual attendance. This was a way of life. The dancefloor was classroom, gallery, church, and community centre.

## Origin Festival — Detailed

- **Location:** Elandskloof Guest Farm, Helderstroom, Western Cape, South Africa
- **Type:** Psytrance gathering
- **Personal significance:** Falls on Ash's birthday weekend every year
- **Cycling tradition:** Has cycled to and from Origin four times (2020, 2022, 2023, 2026)
- **Route:** Woodstock, Cape Town → Sir Lowry's Pass → Grabouw → Elgin Valley → Houwhoek Pass → Helderstroom
- "The pilgrimage to a psytrance festival is as important as the event itself — cycling through mountain passes with a loaded bike is a meditation, a way to cleanse the mind before entering the psychedelic playground. This festival is where my trance family gathers, and arriving on two wheels makes the experience complete."

## Festival Culture — What It Means

### The Dancefloor as Classroom
- Psytrance culture shaped Ash's identity, creative vision, and community
- The dancefloor became both a creative studio and a classroom
- Festival connections led to lifelong friendships across continents
- "When 140 BPM became the heartbeat of understanding"
- The festival is where ADHD brains feel at home — the sensory richness, the freedom from convention, the community of fellow misfits

### Music Preference Evolution
- 25+ years immersed in psytrance — the roots remain
- Heart now beats for **house and techno**
- "I'm really particular about music. I'm a bit over trance so it's gotta be really good trance for me to enjoy it"
- "Times change, people change, and you discover new things"
- Three tracks that map to turning points in life:
  1. First backpacking trip in Thailand from Hua Hin to Phuket — the freedom, the road ahead, the unexpected adventures
  2. The 2001 solar eclipse in Zambia
  3. WordCamp events, specifically WordCamp Europe

### What Makes a Great Psytrance Floor
- **The crowd:** Has a massive impact — "on Koh Phangan I haven't really found that the parties are great, primarily because the crowd doesn't interact"
- **The music:** Has to be really good quality — "it's gotta be really good trance for me to enjoy it"
- **The decor:** Definitely important — "if I'm gonna be taking acid I wanna see some trippy shit"
- **The flow:** Essential — "breaking the music, having technical issues, bad sound system and all of that sort of thing can really put a damper on the party"

### Hearing Protection
- Uses **Loop Experience 2** ear plugs for protecting hearing at festivals and clubs

### Festival Selection Criteria
- In Berlin: "almost guaranteed of a good time when I go to Sisyphos"
- Music curation, people, location, safety, vibe all factor in
- Prefers events where the crowd interacts and the energy is shared

### What Festival Organisers Should Know About Cyclists
- "Give us a free ticket if we cycle long distance"

### Spontaneous Connection
- "The best art happens in the moment. I live for the spontaneous connections on the dancefloor. I look for the radiant souls amplifying the energy. If the vibe matches, we create magic."
- Festival energy moves fast. Working in these conditions — loud, dark, crowded, constantly moving — is what shaped the ambidextrous technique.

### The Global Circuit
- Cape Town, Berlin, Thailand — wherever psytrance plays, Ash paints
- Not glamorous digital nomadism: dusty festival campsites, sleeping on friends' couches, everything you need fits on a bicycle
- "The less you carry, the further you go."

### Types of Art by Region
- **South African crowds:** Bold, expressive colour blocks
- **Berlin clubbers:** Sharp geometric precision, edgier looks that cut through strobe lights
- **Thai festival-goers:** Full-body UV coverage, wilder organic patterns
- **European festivals:** A mix, informed by the culture and energy of each place

### Sacred Space
- "The dancefloor became sacred space"
- "Never about escape — always about deeper engagement with reality"
- Integration through journaling, cycling, creating, and trusted friendships
- "Ego is optional, connection is everything"
