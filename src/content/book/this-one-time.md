## Overview

Ash has decided to write a book over the next few years.

## Title

**"This one time on acid..."**

## Design

- **Cover:** Neon pink
- **Writing/Text:** Neon yellow on atomic black

## Concept

People love Ash's crazy stories. He wants to weave them into his biggest life lessons and deepest learnings — the experiences that changed him, made him who he is, and why they impacted him so profoundly.

**Key theme:** It is never one thing. Usually it is the culmination of many experiences over a period that finally crystallise into understanding.

## Core Threads

### Aquarius + ADHD + Lucy

How Ash's Aquarian crazy soul met with his ADHD neurodiverse brain, how these combined with Lucy (expanded awareness/transformative experiences) made him who he is and helped him achieve what he has to date.

### Story Structure

Each chapter weaves a wild story (the kind friends retell at 4am around a campfire) into life's biggest lessons:
- What the chaos teaches you
- How the wiring that felt like a bug turned out to be the feature
- How the festival dancefloor became the classroom where everything made sense
- How ADHD isn't a disorder when your environment matches your brain's operating system

## Timeline

- Writing over the next few years
- Not to be rushed
- Some chapters already drafted in voice notes and notebook pages
- Others are still being lived
- The book arrives when it's ready, not before

## Chapter Previews (Working Titles)

### Part One — Early Life (Pre-Y2K)
1. **Snails in the Garden** — An only child in a small Afrikaans town, building worlds nobody asked for.
2. **Wired Different** — ADHD, Aquarius, and the feeling that the world was running on a different operating system.
3. **Half Colours** — Racing bicycles, the 2 o'clock club, and finding the first thing that matched the wiring.
4. **The First Drop** — Every journey starts somewhere impossible. This one started on a dancefloor in the Western Cape bush.

### Part Two — Carefree 20s & Early
5. **Eighty-Six Hours** — A bus to Zambia, three border crossings, and three and a half minutes that rearranged everything.
6. **The Costume Evolution** — From the Chicken Man to the Cow Man — and the identity that was building underneath.
7. **The Dancefloor Gave Me Everything** — The people, the connections, the life built on 140 BPM.
8. **LSD** — LightSpeedDevelopment. The acronym was intentional.
9. **Island Time** — Thailand, the island that became a second home, and the road to 7,000 kilometres.

### Part Three — Nomadic Life Begins Before COVID (BC)
10. **Six Cats: The Green Garden** — A garden in Cape Town, nearly 20 years of growing experience, and a cannabis club born in May 2019.
11. **Berlin Calling** — A bicycle, a city discovered in 2019 just before the world stopped, and the freedom to be unapologetically yourself.
12. **The Loaded Bike** — UV paints, brush kit, mirror stand, camping gear, touring panniers. 40 kilograms. Everything an artist needs, on two wheels.
13. **Neon Revelations** — July 2019. The night Ash picked up UV paint and discovered his voice wasn't in words.
14. **The Pilgrimage** — The cycling tradition to Origin Festival — and then the world stopped.

### Part Four — Re-emergence & Finding Myself Again
15. **The Artist's Lifestyle** — Someone at a party told me: "You live an artist's lifestyle." They were right.
16. **Dance Like No One's Watching** — The dancefloor as sacred space, and the art of making people shine.
17. **One Million Steps** — Berlin summer 2025 — the record-breaking season.
18. **Freedom as Operating Principle** — The business, the team, and why freedom isn't the opposite of discipline.
19. **Twenty-Three Years** — LightSpeed at 23: what running a company for more than half your life teaches you.
20. **The Cumulative Effect** — It's never one moment. It's the thousand moments that suddenly click.

### Appendices
- **Appendix A: Dance Like No One's Watching** — The other book or maybe this book's secret twin.
- **Appendix B: The Tribes** — A map of belonging across six continents and six communities.
