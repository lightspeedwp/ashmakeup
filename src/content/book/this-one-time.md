# The Book - "This one time on acid..."

## Overview

Ash has decided to write a book over the next few years.

## Title
**"This one time on acid..."**

## Design
- **Cover:** Neon pink
- **Writing/Text:** Neon yellow

## Concept

People love Ash's crazy stories. He wants to weave them into his biggest life lessons and deepest learnings - the experiences that changed him, made him who he is, and why they impacted him so profoundly.

**Key theme:** It is never one thing. Usually it is the culmination of many experiences over a period that finally crystallise into understanding.

## Core Threads

### Aquarius + ADHD + Lucy
How Ash's Aquarian crazy soul met with his ADHD neurodiverse brain, how these combined with Lucy (expanded awareness/transformative experiences) made him who he is and helped him achieve what he has to date.

### Story Structure
Each chapter weaves a wild story (the kind friends retell at 4am around a campfire) into life's biggest lessons:
- What the chaos teaches you
- How the wiring that felt like a bug turned out to be the feature
- How the festival dancefloor became the classroom where everything made sense
- How ADHD isn't a disorder when your environment matches your brain's operating system

## Timeline
- Writing over the next few years
- Not to be rushed
- Some chapters already drafted in voice notes and notebook pages
- Others are still being lived
- The book arrives when it's ready, not before

## Chapter Previews (Working Titles)

1. **The First Drop** - Every journey starts somewhere impossible. This one started on a rooftop in Cape Town.
2. **Wired Different** - ADHD, Aquarius, and the feeling that the world was running on a different operating system.
3. **Berlin Calling** - A one-way ticket, a bicycle, and the city that would become home.
4. **The Dancefloor Classroom** - When 140 BPM became the heartbeat of understanding.
5. **Neon Revelations** - The night Ash picked up UV paint and discovered his voice wasn't in words.
6. **The Cumulative Effect** - It's never one moment. It's the thousand moments that suddenly click.

## Blurb (Draft)

A raw, honest memoir weaving the wildest stories from the psytrance dancefloor into the deepest lessons they taught. How an Aquarian soul, an ADHD brain, and Lucy in the Sky combined to create an artist, a nomad, and a life lived in full colour.

## Alternative Book Concept — "Dance Like No One's Watching"

Ash has also explored an alternative book title and structure centred on dance and costume evolution:

**Title:** "Dance like no one's watching"

**Chapter Concept:**
1. Early days of dancing
2. The cow suit
3. The chicken suit
4. My rave suit
5. Makeup

This structure traces the visual and identity evolution from the dancefloor outward — costumes as confidence, movement as medicine, and makeup as the final creative expression. Whether this becomes a standalone book or merges with "This one time on acid..." remains to be decided.

## Legacy & What People Will Remember

- **In makeup:** People thanked him years later. An experience where he saw someone after not seeing them for two years — he'd done their makeup two years ago. That day they felt extremely special; everyone kept complimenting them and it made their day.
