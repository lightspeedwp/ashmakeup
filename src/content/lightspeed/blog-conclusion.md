# Blog Post Conclusion - Team & Workflow Update

## Pride in the Team

Ash is incredibly proud of the existing team, the new interns, and the new teammate joining in the coming weeks. LightSpeed has a unique culture - Ash is not the normal employer. He gives the team quite a bit of free reign to decide their own schedules, how they work, and how they live, because work impacts every part of what we do in today's world.

Freedom is something that many employees don't experience. The freedom given to the LightSpeed team helps them lead happier lives and ultimately benefits the company because they deliver better work.

## The Journey to This Point

It's taken a long time and many years of change, adaptation, and adjustment to get to this point. Ash believes that right now LightSpeed has the most optimal workflow and team it has ever had in the 22 years the company has been going.

## Current Pace of Progress

The team is levelling up week on week at a rate never seen before in Ash's career. Yes, this is because of AI, but without a team that has the drive to learn, progress, and grow, this would not happen.

## Looking Ahead

In the coming weeks, further reports on progress, workflow, and strategy moving forward. Things are evolving at a faster pace than ever seen in technology in all of Ash's 25 years in the industry. It's hard to say what the team will be doing even six weeks from now.

Extremely excited about the coming months and the final quarter of 2025. Looking forward to seeing what the projects will hold in the months to come before finishing up this incredible year.
