## The Internship Philosophy

LightSpeed needed developers who were not tainted by the wrong kind of skills. The WordPress industry has a bad reputation because many people use Elementor or other page builders and produce sites that may look good but perform badly.

**The recruitment approach:** 450 LinkedIn applicants. Requirements: complete GitHub skills courses, apply via the LightSpeed website, have an up-to-date LinkedIn profile and GitHub profile. Only 2 made it to interview.

**Why these interns were selected:**
- Showed incredible drive to learn
- Ash saw all the qualities that make a strong LightSpeed developer
- They didn't have past experience — and that was exactly what was wanted
- Experienced developers often think they know better and don't listen
