# Sub-audit 01: Voice & tone audit

**Date:** March 3, 2026
**Status:** Complete

---

## Summary

The ebook has strong authentic voice in Parts 1–2 (early life, festivals) but degrades significantly in Parts 3–4 where large blocks of website-content.md were transplanted without voice adaptation. Two chapters are critical offenders.

## Critical issues (P0 — rewrite required)

### Ch 10: Six Cats (part-3.ts, pages 47–57)
- **Cat bios** (ch10-content-2, ch10-content-3): Written in formal third-person energy. "He arrived as a stray, malnourished and cautious" reads like an adoption profile, not a memoir. Ash talks about these cats with love and humour — the bios should sound like a guy introducing his weird family, not a shelter listing.
- **Values section** (ch10-content-5, ch10-content-6): Lifted almost verbatim from sixcats.club website copy. "Every harvest season, these eight values guide the operation" is corporate language. A memoir would say something like "I didn't write these values down until someone asked. But they were always there."
- **Cultivation methods** (ch10-content-7, ch10-content-8): Reads like a product brochure. "We build soil, we don't buy it" is decent, but the surrounding text is too instructional. Needs to feel like someone explaining their obsession, not a how-to guide.
- **Grading system** (ch10-content-9): Pure website copy. "Quads (AAAA): Top-shelf. Dense, trichome-covered..." — this is catalogue language, not memoir prose.

### Ch 19: Twenty-three years (part-4.ts, pages 88–97)
- **Timeline section** (ch19-content-2 through ch19-content-4): Information dump. A chronological list of hire dates feels like an HR document. The human stories are buried under facts.
- **Products & open source** (ch19-content-7): Reads like a README. "LSX Design System: Our flagship design system built in Figma" is product marketing copy.
- **AI & modern workflow** (ch19-content-8): Overly polished. "The fusion of AI, creativity, and open source has been a radical, life-changing shift" appears verbatim in multiple places across the codebase — it's become a stock phrase.
- **What twenty-three years teaches you** (ch19-content-9): The lesson format ("Freedom requires structure. The right people change everything.") is borderline self-help listicle.

## High issues (P1 — needs voice polish)

### Ch 5: Eighty-six hours (part-2.ts)
- **ch5-content-3** (border crossings): The three-border expansion is vivid but over-literary. "The mist, the roar, the sense that nature was doing something magnificent nearby" — Ash would more likely say "you could hear the falls before you could see them." Needs grounding.
- **ch5-content-4** (totality): The eclipse description is beautiful but tips into purple prose. "streamers of plasma reaching into space" is more Carl Sagan than Ash Shaw.

### Ch 6: Costume evolution (part-2.ts)
- **ch6-content-3**: The year-by-year timeline format breaks the narrative flow. Instead of "1999–2000: The yellow suit. Chicken Man era." this should be woven into storytelling.

### Ch 9: Island time (part-2.ts)
- **ch9-content-3, ch9-content-4**: Over-elaborated. The cycling route descriptions repeat information already established. The Koh Phangan daily routine section reads like a travel brochure.

### Ch 15: The artist's lifestyle (part-4.ts)
- **ch15-content-3**: Month-by-month calendar format breaks the memoir voice. This reads like a lifestyle blog post, not a book chapter.

## Moderate issues (P2 — polish when possible)

- Ch 7 content 3-4: South African trance scene description is good content but slightly Wikipedia-like
- Ch 13 content 4: Technique discoveries section is strong but the "co-creation philosophy" paragraph tips into artist-statement language
- Appendix B (tribes): Acceptable for appendix format but some entries are generic

- Ch 2: "It's not a deficit of attention. It's a surplus of it" — quintessential Ash
- Ch 4: "By morning they were calling me the Chicken Man" — punchy, real
- Ch 5 content 1-2: Zambia bus story core — genuine storytelling
- Ch 7 content 1: Meeting Barbara — raw and honest
- Ch 8: Entire chapter — compact, authentic, funny ("The acronym is LSD. That was intentional.")
- Ch 17: One million steps — brief, joyful, real
